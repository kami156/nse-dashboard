6. Technical Analysis Engine

Indicators: RSI, MACD, EMA 20/50/100/200, VWAP (20d proxy), Supertrend, ADX, ATR, Bollinger, pivots, volume ratio. Technical Score 0–100.

How to use

  1. Trend first — Uptrend means price is above rising EMAs, Downtrend the opposite. Trade with the trend.
  2. RSI 14: above 70 is overbought (pullback risk), below 30 oversold (bounce potential); 45–65 is healthy.
  3. MACD Hist positive = bullish momentum, negative = bearish; watch for sign flips as trend shifts.
  4. EMA stack: Price > EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 signals strong bullish alignment.
  5. ADX: below 18 is weak/choppy, 18–25 moderate, 25+ a strong trend (only trust the trend above 25).
  6. ATR gauges volatility — use it to size stops; larger ATR means wider, safer stop distance.
  7. Supertrend: price above the level is bullish, below is bearish — a useful trailing stop.
  8. Tech Score 0–100: 65+ bullish, 45–64 neutral, under 45 bearish. Click the column header to rank.

7. Fundamental Analysis Engine

Per-stock FII/DII ownership is unavailable; the FII/DII column shows market-level cash activity when NSE feeds succeed. Fundamentals/valuations still come from yfinance. ROCE approximated via operating quality metrics where ROCE is missing.

How to use

  1. Fund Score = quality & growth (revenue/EPS growth, ROE, margins, FCF, promoter conviction). 65+ is strong.
  2. Val Score = valuation (P/E, P/B). Higher means cheaper relative to earnings/book.
  3. Pair them — high Fund + high Val is quality at a reasonable price; low Fund + high Val can be a value trap.
  4. ROE 15%+ and Op Margin 10%+ point to a profitable, efficient business.
  5. D/E: below 0.5 is healthy, above 1.5 is leveraged (higher risk).
  6. Promoter 40%+ signals strong insider conviction.
  7. FII/DII column is market-level cash activity, not per-stock ownership — treat it as context, not a stock signal.
